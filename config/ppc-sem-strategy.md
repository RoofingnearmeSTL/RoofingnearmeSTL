# PPC & SEM Strategy for RoofingnearmeSTL

## Overview
This document outlines the pay-per-click and search engine marketing strategy to complement the organic SEO efforts.

### Target Keywords by Intent

#### High Intent (Buyer Keywords)
- "roof repair near me"
- "emergency roof repair St. Louis"
- "roof replacement St. Louis"
- "storm damage roof St. Louis"
- "roofer near me 63101" (zip code targeting)
- "best roofer in St. Louis"
- "affordable roof repair"
- "roof repair quotes"

#### Commercial Keywords
- "commercial roofing St. Louis"
- "commercial roof repair"
- "flat roof replacement"

#### Seasonal Keywords
- "spring roof inspection"
- "storm season roofing"
- "winter roof damage"
- "hail damage roof repair"

### Platform Strategy

#### Google Ads (PPC)
**Budget Allocation:** 40-50% of digital budget
- Search Ads: High-intent keywords, local targeting
- Performance Max: Dynamic ads across Google properties
- Local Services Ads: "Get quotes" buttons, verified ratings
- Geographic Targeting: St. Louis metro area, 30-mile radius
- Bid Strategy: Target ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) 3:1
- Landing Pages: Direct to service pages + lead form

**Ad Copy Focus:**
- Urgency: "Same-day emergency service"
- Trust: "Licensed, insured, 20-year warranty"
- Offer: "Free inspection & estimate"
- Social proof: "500+ projects completed, 4.9★ rating"

#### Google Local Services Ads
**Priority: HIGH** (Perfect for roofing)
- Display "Get quotes" button
- Show customer reviews and ratings
- Google verifies license and insurance
- Pay only when customer contacts you

#### Microsoft Ads (Bing/Yahoo)
**Budget Allocation:** 10-15% of digital budget
- Less competitive than Google
- Often lower cost-per-click
- Older demographic (still valuable in home services)

#### Facebook & Instagram Ads
**Budget Allocation:** 20-25% of digital budget
- Retargeting: Website visitors who didn't convert
- Lookalike audiences: Similar to past leads
- Local targeting: St. Louis metro area
- Ad formats: Before/after carousel, testimonial videos
- Audiences: Homeowners 35+, high household income

#### YouTube Ads
**Budget Allocation:** 10-15% of digital budget
- In-stream ads: Roof repair/replacement process videos
- Discovery ads: Roof-related searches
- Target keywords: "how to repair roof", "roof replacement"

### Conversion Tracking
- Install Google Analytics 4 on website
- Track: Form submissions, phone calls, page visits
- Implement UTM parameters for campaign tracking
- Set up conversion value: $X per lead

### Landing Page Strategy
Create dedicated landing pages for PPC campaigns:
- `/campaign/emergency-roof-repair` - for emergency repair ads
- `/campaign/roof-replacement` - for replacement ads
- `/campaign/storm-damage` - for seasonal campaigns
- Each page: Fast loading, clear CTA, minimal navigation, social proof

### A/B Testing
- Test ad headlines, descriptions, CTAs
- Test landing page layouts and forms
- Optimize based on conversion rate
- Focus on reducing Cost Per Lead (CPL)

### Budget Recommendation
Monthly Budget: $2,000 - $5,000
- Google Ads (Local + Search): $1,000 - $2,500
- Local Services Ads: $300 - $500
- Microsoft Ads: $300 - $500
- Social Media Retargeting: $400 - $1,000
- YouTube/Video: $200 - $500

### Expected Performance (Conservative Estimates)
- Average Cost Per Lead: $25 - $50
- Conversion Rate: 8-12% (leads to customers)
- Customer Lifetime Value: $5,000 - $15,000
- ROI: 10-25x initial investment

### Monthly Optimization Tasks
1. Review keyword performance
2. Pause underperforming ads
3. Increase bids on high-converting keywords
4. Test new ad copy and CTAs
5. Analyze landing page performance
6. Adjust budget allocation based on ROI
