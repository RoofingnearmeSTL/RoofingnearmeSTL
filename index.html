Common Questions

How long do roofs typically last?

Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20-30 years with proper maintenance. Premium materials can last longer. We'll assess your roof during the free inspection.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Yes! We assist with insurance claims for storm damage and other covered incidents. We can work directly with your insurance company.

What warranty do you offer?

We provide a 20-year workmanship warranty on all our installations and repairs. We stand behind our work completely.

How quickly can you respond to emergencies?

Are you licensed and insured?
